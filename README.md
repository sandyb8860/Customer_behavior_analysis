# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📊 Objectives

This project aims to:

- **Understand Customer Spending Patterns**: Analyze purchase behavior across different customer segments to identify spending trends and patterns
- **Identify High-Value Customers**: Segment customers based on purchase frequency, average order value, and lifetime value to prioritize retention efforts
- **Optimize Marketing Strategy**: Develop data-driven insights to create targeted marketing campaigns and personalized customer experiences
- **Improve Customer Retention**: Analyze churn patterns and subscription behavior to reduce customer attrition
- **Drive Revenue Growth**: Identify opportunities for upselling, cross-selling, and product optimization based on customer preferences

---

## 📈 Business Metrics Tracked

### Revenue Metrics
- **Total Revenue**: Aggregate sales performance across all transactions
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Mean transaction value per customer purchase
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Projected revenue from each customer over their relationship with the business

### Customer Behavior Metrics
- **Purchase Frequency**: Number of transactions per customer over time
- **Churn Rate**: Percentage of customers who stopped making purchases
- **Subscription Impact**: Spending comparison between subscribed vs. non-subscribed customers
- **Customer Segmentation**: Loyalty tiers based on purchase behavior (high-value, at-risk, new customers)

### Product Performance Metrics
- **Category Revenue**: Sales performance across different product categories
- **Product Preferences**: Top-selling items and category trends
- **Geographic Distribution**: Revenue and spending patterns by location

---

## 💡 Key Business Recommendations

### 1. Prioritize Subscription Program Expansion
**Finding**: Subscribed customers spend 2.5× more than non-subscribers

**Recommendation**: 
- Launch targeted campaigns to convert non-subscribed customers
- Offer introductory subscription discounts or trials
- Highlight subscription benefits (exclusive deals, free shipping, loyalty rewards)

### 2. Implement Tiered Loyalty Program
**Finding**: Identified distinct customer segments with varying spending patterns

**Recommendation**:
- Create VIP programs for high-value customers with exclusive perks
- Design re-engagement campaigns for at-risk customers
- Develop welcome series for new customers to boost early retention

### 3. Optimize Product Mix and Inventory
**Finding**: Clear product category preferences identified across customer segments

**Recommendation**:
- Increase inventory for top-performing categories
- Bundle complementary products based on purchase patterns
- Phase out or discount underperforming items

### 4. Personalize Marketing by Geographic Location
**Finding**: Spending patterns vary significantly by location

**Recommendation**:
- Tailor promotions to regional preferences
- Adjust pricing strategies based on local market conditions
- Optimize shipping and fulfillment for high-value regions

### 5. Reduce Churn Through Predictive Interventions
**Finding**: Analyzed churn trends and identified early warning signals

**Recommendation**:
- Implement automated alerts for customers showing churn risk indicators
- Deploy win-back campaigns with personalized offers
- Conduct exit surveys to understand reasons for customer departure
